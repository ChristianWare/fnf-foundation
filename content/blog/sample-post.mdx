Posts are Markdown files in `content/blog/`. The filename is the URL slug, and the frontmatter at the top of the file provides the title, date, excerpt, tags, and an optional related link to one money page.

## What to write

One post answers one question a rider searches before booking. Start with pricing ("How much does a car service to the airport cost?"), then comparisons (black car vs. rideshare), then local guides (airport pickup instructions, the best wedding venues in the metro), then trust (how chauffeurs are vetted).

## Formatting

Use `##` headings for each subquestion. Tables and links work. Set `draft: true` to hide a post from the site and the sitemap.
